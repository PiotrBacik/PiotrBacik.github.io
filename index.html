About me

Since October 2024, I am a DPhil student on the Oxford-Max-Planck Doctoral Program (joint with MPI-SWS in Saarbrücken) under the supervision of Joël Ouaknine and James Worrell.

My research interests lie mainly in linear dynamical systems. In particular, I'm interested in the Skolem Problem and other questions surrounding linear recurrence sequences and their generaliseations.

Previously, I completed a Bachelor's and Master's in Mathematics at the University of Cambridge.

I'm in Oxford from October 2026 to the end of June 2027.
